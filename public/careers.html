Technical Positions

Solar & Battery Installers

Deploy advanced solar and battery systems across Southeast Queensland. Clean technical jobs with no sales involvement—just quality installations at scale.

  • Install 10kW solar arrays with 40-50kWh battery systems
  • Commission VPP-integrated energy systems
  • Ensure compliance with AS4777 and AS5033 standards
  • Progress through Pi's internal certification program (Levels 1-3)
  • Scale opportunities as Pi expands nationally
Requirements:
  • SAA accreditation (solar & battery)
  • Clean Energy Council Design & Install certification (or equivalent)
  • Minimum 2 years installation experience
  • Strong safety culture and attention to detail
  • Own tools and reliable transport

What You Get: Job certainty, clarity on expectations, technical focus without sales pressure, and genuine scale opportunities as Pi rolls out across Australia.

Site Inspectors

Ideal for professionals seeking consistent field work without the demands of larger installation jobs. Conduct 5-7 comprehensive pre-installation assessments daily, focused on safety, compliance, and performance optimization.

  • Check existing electrical infrastructure suitability
  • Assess installation location compliance
  • Calculate system performance capabilities
  • Prepare detailed inspection reports
  • Direct customer interface—no liaison with installers or designers
Requirements:
  • Strong understanding of safety, compliance, and performance requirements for energy asset design and deployment
  • Systematic approach to inspection methodology
  • Excellent communication and reporting skills
  • Reliable transport and ability to manage daily schedule
  • Understanding of AS/NZS 3000 (Wiring Rules) beneficial but not essential

What You Get: Predictable daily workflow, clear scope of work, no crossover with installation teams, and steady volume as Pi scales.

Service Electricians

Comprehensive electrical services supporting Pi's energy asset deployments. Clean technical work with job certainty and clear scope.

  • Meter board upgrades and relocations
  • Backup circuit installations
  • Troubleshooting and diagnostics
  • Preventative maintenance programs
  • Prep works for system installations
  • System performance optimization
Requirements:
  • Licensed electrician (A-grade preferred)
  • Current electrical contractor license and insurance
  • Experience with renewable energy systems beneficial
  • Diagnostic and problem-solving expertise
  • Availability for scheduled service and emergency callouts

What You Get: Diversified electrical work, no sales involvement, consistent workflow, and expansion opportunities as Pi's asset base grows.

VPP Operations Specialist

Optimize Pi's Virtual Power Plant and peer-to-peer energy marketplace—the platform where retailers, aggregators, installers, and customers all benefit.

  • Monitor and optimize VPP performance across Pi's asset fleet
  • Coordinate wholesale market participation and arbitrage
  • Manage peer-to-peer energy trading operations
  • Analyze system data and performance metrics
  • Coordinate with retail partners and market operators
Requirements:
  • Electrical engineering, energy systems, or market operations background
  • Understanding of NEM operations and wholesale energy markets
  • Data analysis and optimization capabilities
  • Experience with energy management systems or market platforms
  • Strategic thinking and commercial awareness

What You Get: Central role in Pi's platform operations, exposure to cutting-edge energy trading, and career growth with Australia's leading distributed energy innovator.
