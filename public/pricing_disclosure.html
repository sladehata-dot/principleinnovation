Transparent, Simple, and Comprehensive Energy Pricing

Energy Pricing Structure

Fixed Rate Guarantee

Your energy is supplied at a simple, transparent anytime rate of $0.26 per kilowatt-hour (kWh). In addition, property supply charges (daily network/supply fees applicable to your property) are passed through to you at cost with zero mark-up. This means:

  • All energy consumption measured at your property at $0.26/kWh — anytime, no peak/off-peak complexity
  • Property supply and network charges passed through at the exact cost Pi is charged — no margin added
  • No environmental scheme charge mark-ups
  • No seasonal or time-of-use variations on the energy rate

Energy is supplied under retail authorization held by Virtual Watt, with billing presented under Pi Energy branding.

How Pi Delivers This Rate

Following your Energy Intelligence Audit (EIA), Pi designs and installs a solar and battery system specifically sized for your property's energy requirements. Our systems are intentionally designed with substantial capacity margins to ensure consistent supply and energy security.

Example: If your EIA shows 20 kWh average daily consumption, Pi might install:

  • 10 kW solar array
  • 40-50 kWh battery storage capacity

This approach provides substantial energy autonomy for your property. Surplus energy generated by your system is aggregated into Pi's Virtual Power Plant (VPP) and peer-to-peer (P2P) energy marketplace, generating additional revenue that supports the $0.26/kWh anytime rate.

Supply Continuity and Grid Independence

Pi's system design prioritizes on-site energy generation and storage. In the unlikely event that your property's energy demand temporarily exceeds on-site capacity, Pi sources additional supply through its VPP and P2P distributed energy marketplace at the same $0.26/kWh anytime rate.

Due to the substantial capacity margin built into your system design, grid reliance is minimal under normal operating conditions.

Essential Load Protection

Your installation includes a dedicated backup circuit supporting essential loads during any supply disruptions:

  • Food preservation (refrigeration)
  • Lighting
  • Communications equipment

This backup capability provides energy security superior to grid-only supply, particularly during network outages or extreme weather events.

Consumption Monitoring and Optimization

Pi continuously monitors your energy consumption patterns to optimize system performance and marketplace operations. If your consumption increases significantly above your EIA assessment, Pi's intelligent systems automatically balance supply through our VPP/P2P marketplace while maintaining your $0.26/kWh anytime rate.

Your consumption notifications:

  • Real-time monitoring through your customer portal
  • Proactive notifications if usage patterns change
  • Energy efficiency recommendations to maximize your system benefits
  • No penalty for increased consumption—Pi's marketplace model benefits from higher energy throughput

This monitoring ensures you always have reliable supply while Pi optimizes revenue from our distributed energy network.

Force Majeure and Extraordinary Circumstances

The $0.26/kWh anytime rate applies under all normal operating conditions. In extraordinary circumstances beyond Pi's reasonable control, temporary supply disruptions or rate variations may occur:

Force Majeure Events Include:

  • Extreme weather events causing transmission network failures
  • Natural disasters affecting energy infrastructure
  • Government-mandated supply restrictions or emergency directions
  • Other events of similar unforeseen and uncontrollable nature

What This Means for You:

Due to the emergency nature of force majeure events, advance notice is not possible. However, your on-site solar and battery assets provide a critical advantage during such events:

  • Grid-connected customers: Experience complete power outages with no backup capability
  • Pi Energy customers: Maintain power to essential loads (refrigeration, lighting, communications) through your dedicated backup circuit, even when the broader network is down

Any rate variations resulting from force majeure events will be communicated as soon as practicable, with clear explanation of:

  • The nature of the event
  • Expected duration of any variations
  • Steps being taken to restore normal service
  • Your rights and options under National Energy Retail Law

Pi will make all reasonable efforts to minimize customer impact during force majeure events and restore standard $0.26/kWh anytime rate pricing as quickly as possible.

Rate Stability Track Record

Pi's intelligent system design approach is anticipated to maintain rate stability due to:

  1. Conservative EIA methodology ensuring accurate consumption assessment
  2. System design with substantial capacity margins
  3. VPP/P2P marketplace optimization balancing supply and demand across our network
  4. Daily wholesale market reconciliation ensuring cost efficiency

The primary operational focus for Pi is optimizing surplus energy generation through our marketplace—system capacity is designed to substantially exceed individual household requirements under normal conditions.

Your Consumer Protections

Energy is supplied under Virtual Watt's retail authorization, providing you with full protections under:

  • National Energy Retail Law (NERL)
  • National Energy Retail Rules (NERR)
  • Australian Consumer Law (ACL)

Virtual Watt extends all legally required consumer protections to Pi Energy customers, including:

  • Energy Ombudsman dispute resolution
  • Hardship provisions and payment assistance programs
  • Clear billing and consumption information
  • Contract variation protections
  • Guaranteed Service Levels

Regulatory Transparency

This pricing structure is made possible through:

  1. Pi's ownership and operation of on-site energy assets (solar and battery systems)
  2. White-label retail arrangement with Virtual Watt (AER authorized retailer)
  3. VPP and P2P marketplace revenue optimization
  4. Daily wholesale market reconciliation between Pi and Virtual Watt

This arrangement provides you with the consumer protections of traditional retail energy supply while delivering the benefits of distributed energy resources and innovative marketplace optimization.
