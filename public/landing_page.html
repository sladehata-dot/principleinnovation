Simple 6-Step Journey

1

Eligibility Confirmation

We verify your property location (Gold Coast to Brisbane), ownership status, and system requirements. You'll receive a personalized savings estimate and system recommendation.

2

Energy Bill Analysis

Upload your recent bills (PDF or images). Our AI-powered tool analyzes your consumption patterns, rates, and usage data to determine your eligibility and potential savings. Instant result.

3

Site Inspection - $297

Safety, compliance and performance assessment of your property and any existing energy systems. We check electrical infrastructure, verify installation requirements, and assess current solar/battery equipment (if present). Important: Infrastructure upgrades or existing system remediation may be required at your cost. Where existing systems have defects, Pi may offer replacement at no cost, but removal and disposal costs apply. Inspection fee fully credited to your monthly energy bill once operational. Detailed inspection report provided.

4

Custom Design & Engineering

Our engineers design your system and submit network connection application to Energex. We manage the entire approval process so you don't have to. You're kept informed every step.

5

Professional Installation

SAA-accredited installers complete your system to Australian Standards AS4777 and AS5033. Energy infrastructure, monitoring equipment, and safety systems—everything designed for energy security and energy price certainty.

6

Activation & Ongoing Support

System connected to Pi's 24/7 monitoring platform and VPP network. Start generating and storing clean energy immediately. Pay only $0.26/kWh anytime rate for energy consumed, plus property supply charges at cost — no mark-up.
